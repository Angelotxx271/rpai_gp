Claims are paraphrased; open the link for the exact wording. "Partly" means the source supports the claim with the correction noted. This is market evidence: it shows what exists, not what IE students do (see market-research.md for behaviour evidence)

Strength: strong = official or primary source, recent; medium = reputable secondary or older; weak = vendor marketing, blog or undated

Some rows give a figure whose framing the check corrects (for example #38: the 89% is the share of AI-using students who use chat assistants, as #166 states). Where the check column says "partly", use the corrected reading
