# Opportunity

## Pain point
Students spend weeks or months of work before detecting that what they're doing doesn't fulfil the requirements or scope of their topic; it can happen at any stage of the process, and it hurts the process or stops them delivering on time.

## User, need, and insight
Final-year IE students doing their TFG, any degree, need to realise, while working between supervisor meetings, when their work stops fulfilling the requirements or scope of their topic, instead of weeks or months later, because students will use AI no matter what, but they're more concerned with making the work look not-AI than with using it well, even as IE moves to "disclose it, no penalty".

## How might we
How might we help final-year IE students detect and avoid drift from their TFG's requirements and scope during the long stretches of work between supervisor meetings?
