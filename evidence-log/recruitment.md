# Recruitment (test access)

Not on the Session 5 folder tree, but every course slide footer says "Test access: recruitment.md", and the reviewing team's first question is "who are you testing this on?".

## Who we test with
Final-year IE bachelor students doing their TFG, from our own cohort, any degree. Mix of students who have started and who haven't; note which on the observation sheet.

## How many
10 tested (the threshold is 6 of 10). Recruit 12 to cover no-shows. No decision on a partial sample.

## How we reach them
Class WhatsApp group plus direct messages, and the TFGPT landing page (tfgpt-production.up.railway.app): shared in the class WhatsApp group, its signups answer whether they'd use it instead of, alongside, or probably not with their own AI, and signups become the invite list. One 25-minute slot per student, in person or on Teams with screen share. Two days before each slot: ask for degree, assigned topic/line, whether they've started, and optional notes or draft.

## By when
Sessions: Monday 28 September to Friday 2 October 2026. Results and decision-rule outcome recorded in experiment-card.md by: Monday 5 October 2026, before the Phase 1 gate at Session 15 (15 October).

## Consent
The brief says it's an early pilot and that part of it is operated manually. Full reveal after the session. Any thesis text shared is deleted after the session unless the student agrees otherwise.
