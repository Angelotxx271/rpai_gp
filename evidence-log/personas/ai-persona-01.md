# Persona: The AI Cross-Checker

**Status:** Selected critical Persona. Partly checked with the person it is based on (a stated intention only); not validated with other users

**Source draft:** drafts/persona-hypothesis-01.md. Updated 2026-09-21 from interview-evidence.md 03

## Shared Job to Be Done

When final-year IE students work on their TFG through two-month gaps between deliverable meetings without knowing whether they're on track, finding out at a meeting that the last two months of work were faulty prompts them to seek a way to go into each meeting already knowing their work meets what's required, so they can move on to the next phase

## Behavioural archetype

A final-year student who runs almost all of her work through AI. She feeds it the professor's full instructions and rubric, asks it to confirm the result meets them, and also tries to understand what it did

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Final-year Data student at IE who hasn't started her TFG yet and uses Claude as her main AI
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Double-checks everything with AI (emails, code, projects, papers) across subjects, and wants to understand the AI's output rather than just take it
- **Current approach or alternative:** Pastes every instruction, guideline and rubric from the professor into the AI and tells it to follow them, then asks the same AI whether the work meets the criteria; also reads and checks it herself
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** On new or complex topics she leans on the AI even more, so her own check is weakest where the material is new. The AI that drafts her work is also the one that checks it
- **Product or research decision affected:** Every answer shows which sources and tools it used and where each piece of information comes from, instead of text with no references
