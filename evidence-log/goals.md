# Goals

## Product Goal
Enable final-year IE students to realise when their TFG stops fulfilling its requirements or scope while working between deliverable meetings, so that they don't lose weeks or months and deliver on time.

## Current Sprint Goal
Learn whether final-year IE students actually do their TFG work in this tool instead of their AI of choice, strongly enough to guide the next decision.

Status (2026-10-12): tested with the Wizard-of-Oz pilot. 6 of 10 did the tasks in the chat without opening their own AI, exactly the threshold, so CONTINUE (experiment-card.md). ERRC.md scopes "instead" to the pre-meeting check: students keep their own AI for everything else. The next Sprint Goal is the team's decision.

## Previous Sprint Goal (v0)
Learn whether final-year IE students would use a university-proposed tool for their TFG instead of their AI of choice, and actively engage with it, strongly enough to guide the next decision.
