# Goals

## Product Goal
Enable final-year IE students to realise when their TFG stops fulfilling its requirements or scope while working between deliverable meetings, so that they don't lose weeks or months and deliver on time.

## Current Sprint Goal
Enable final-year IE students (starting with Data and Business Analytics Full Proposals) to realise when their TFG stops fulfilling its requirements or scope before deliverable meetings — testing whether TFGPT coexists alongside their own AI strongly enough that they complete checks in TFGPT and revise their work because of cited flags.

Status (2026-11-26): updated per Angelotxx271 to align the Sprint Goal directly with the Product Goal and the 2026-11-24 riskiest assumption (`students' own AI and TFGPT will coexist`, `assumptions.md`). Validated via the Phase 1 `coexist` user-testing rounds (`specs/roadmap.md`, `evidence-log/user-feedback.md`).

## Previous Sprint Goal (v0.1, 2026-09-21 to 2026-10-12)
Learn whether final-year IE students actually do their TFG work in this tool instead of their AI of choice, strongly enough to guide the next decision.

Status (2026-10-12): tested with the Wizard-of-Oz pilot. 6 of 10 did the tasks in the chat without opening their own AI, exactly the threshold, so CONTINUE (experiment-card.md). ERRC.md scopes "instead" to the pre-meeting check: students keep their own AI for everything else.

## Previous Sprint Goal (v0)
Learn whether final-year IE students would use a university-proposed tool for their TFG instead of their AI of choice, and actively engage with it, strongly enough to guide the next decision.
