# Mission

Version 0.1, 2026-11-23. Status: draft, team review pending

## Purpose
Help final-year IE students know, before each deliverable meeting, whether their TFG still fits its requirements and scope [Evidence-supported: jtbd.md]. Today some find out at the meeting and redo the work [Evidence-supported, second-hand: interview-evidence.md 01]. Why now: it's an ongoing problem that we, as fourth-year students, have experienced first hand and can now do something about [Team decision: own experience]

## Promise
- Like an advisor, it flags problems and suggests possible routes; every answer and flag cites its source [Team decision: ERRC.md]
- Their programme's guidelines are already loaded, starting with Data and Business Analytics: easier to test, documents collected [Team decision: ERRC.md]
- Their topic and work are kept between sessions [Team decision: ERRC.md]

## Non-goals
- No grades or overall verdict: the supervisor decides [Team decision: ERRC.md]
- No drafting or doing the work: the student owns the text [Team decision: ERRC.md]
- Not replacing students' own AI beyond this check [Team decision: market-research.md 7]
- No supervisor view yet: untested with advisors [Team decision: ERRC.md]
- No self-starting checks yet: a later step [Team decision]
