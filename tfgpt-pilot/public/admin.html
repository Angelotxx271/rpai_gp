    TFGPT pilot · your 20 minutes

    Thanks for helping. This is an early pilot of a TFG helper the university might offer. You'll do three real things for your own TFG. Use whatever you normally use: Google, your own AI, the TFGPT chat, anything. Nothing is graded and there are no right answers.

    1. Check whether your project idea (or the idea you're considering) fits the topic or line you've been assigned. Write the answer and one reason.
    2. Find out the structure your TFG must have (the required sections) and one format requirement.
    3. Find the key dates for this year's TFG deliverables.

    Afterwards we'll ask you two short questions about how it went. Part of this pilot is operated manually; we'll explain at the end.

    Before the session
    • Two days before: ask for degree, assigned topic or line, whether they've started, and optional notes or draft.
    • Load the official IE TFG guidelines for their degree. Verify dates and sections in the actual document, not from memory.
    • Prepare the three templates (fit, structure, dates) with the guideline sections to cite.
    • Open this console and the observation sheet. A second person observes if possible; otherwise screen share, with consent.
    During
    • Reply within 60 seconds. Draft with your own AI if you want, then check every claim against the guidelines before sending.
    • Answer only from the guidelines and the student's material, and cite the section ("Guidelines §3.2 Structure").
    • Not in the guidelines: use "Not in the guidelines".
    • Never invent a date. Never write thesis text. Never grade or approve: "this fits" is a check against the topic, not an approval.
    • Tone: a good supervisor in a hurry. Short, specific, one clarifying question at most.
    • Don't prompt them to use the chat. If they leave for their own AI, let them: that is the data.
    After
    • Reveal: "Some answers were prepared by a person using the guidelines. Thanks for testing."
    • Ask: (1) What did you use for each of the three tasks, and why? (2) Would you use this next week for your real work, instead of, or alongside, your AI?
    • Fill the observation sheet now, while you remember the minute they switched.
    • Close the session: it deletes the thesis text unless the student agrees to keep it.
