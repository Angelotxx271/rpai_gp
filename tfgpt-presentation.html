Questions we expect

Who pays?

Untested. Free for students only if IE pays: a kill criterion.

Nobody uses IE AI Engine. Why you?

Students distrust outdated models. We run top models and need IE's commitment.

Isn't this an AI grader? EU AI Act?

No grades or verdicts, only cited flags. Legal position unknown: needs advice.

Are your personas real?

AI-built from our evidence, not validated. The advisor one rests on 2 real advisors.

What did evidence change?

Instead for the check, alongside otherwise · cited flags, no grades · gap varies by programme.

My programme meets monthly. Two months?

That's the CS programme we heard about. The check runs before each programme's dates.

What did advisors say?

~10 TFGs, a few minutes each, ~2 weeks to first reply. Wants to see progress: a wish, not behaviour.

What would make you stop?

Any of the four kill criteria, starting with the head-to-head vs a general AI.
